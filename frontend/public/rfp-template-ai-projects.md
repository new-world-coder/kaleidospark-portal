## Evaluation Criteria

### Scoring Methodology
**Total Score: 100 points**

**Technical Approach (40 points)**
- Solution architecture and design (15 points)
- AI/ML methodology and capabilities (15 points)
- Security and compliance measures (10 points)

**Company Qualifications (25 points)**
- Relevant experience and expertise (15 points)
- Team qualifications and certifications (10 points)

**Project Management (20 points)**
- Project methodology and approach (10 points)
- Risk management and quality assurance (10 points)

**Commercial Terms (15 points)**
- Pricing competitiveness (10 points)
- Value proposition and ROI (5 points)

### Evaluation Process
1. **Initial Review**: Technical and commercial compliance check
2. **Detailed Evaluation**: Scoring against evaluation criteria
3. **Reference Checks**: Verification of client references
4. **Technical Presentation**: Vendor presentations and demos
5. **Final Selection**: Award decision and contract negotiation
