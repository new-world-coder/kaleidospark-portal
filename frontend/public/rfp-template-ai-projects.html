Evaluation Criteria

Scoring Methodology

Total Score: 100 points

Category Points Description
Technical Approach 40 points Solution architecture, AI/ML methodology, security measures
Company Qualifications 25 points Experience, expertise, team qualifications
Project Management 20 points Methodology, risk management, quality assurance
Commercial Terms 15 points Pricing competitiveness, value proposition

Evaluation Process

  1. Initial Review: Technical and commercial compliance check
  2. Detailed Evaluation: Scoring against evaluation criteria
  3. Reference Checks: Verification of client references
  4. Technical Presentation: Vendor presentations and demos
  5. Final Selection: Award decision and contract negotiation
